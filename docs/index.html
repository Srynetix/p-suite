Prism

Your merge requests, tickets, pipelines, clusters and AI agents in one local, real-time dashboard, sorted by what needs you.

A developer's day is spread over a dozen tabs: the forge, the tracker, CI, the cluster, the calendar, the agents at work. Prism reads them all, live, and answers one question first: what needs me now?

  • One window, every source

    GitLab or GitHub, ClickUp, Jira or Obsidian, Kubernetes and ArgoCD, your calendar and mail, Claude Code sessions.

  • Drift, caught for you

    A red pipeline on your branch, a ticket still “doing” while its MR is in review, an agent stuck on a permission: all in Alerts, most severe first.

  • Local, by design

    No account and no cloud. Prism reuses the access you already have, gh, glab, your kubeconfig and keychain.

Pastille

An electronics simulator for people who have never built a circuit, and the bridge from a circuit that works on screen to one that works on your desk.

Most circuit simulators are built for people who already know what a node voltage is. Pastille is for the person who just bought a starter kit and wonders why the LED did not light up.

  • See electricity move

    Dots flow with the current, every wire takes the colour of its voltage, from blue at 0 V to pink at the supply.

  • Mistakes, explained

    An LED the wrong way round or a short across the battery: Pastille says what would happen on a real bench, and how to fix it.

  • 56 parts and a real Arduino

    From a battery to a 555 and a shift register, plus an Arduino Uno that runs a real compiled sketch. 111 examples to start from.

Pavé

Pave the way for your windows. A window manager for macOS that brings the switcher, snapping, taskbar and workspaces you miss.

Switched to a Mac after years on Windows or Linux? Your hands still reach for Alt+Tab and throw windows against the screen edges. Pavé gives them what they expect, without fighting the Mac you chose.

  • Every window, one keystroke away

    ⌘Tab shows every window, not every app, with live previews. Type to search.

  • Snap, zones and tiling

    Halves and quarters on the arrows, FancyZones-style layouts for wide screens, tiling when you want it.

  • A real taskbar and workspaces

    A taskbar with hover previews, and workspaces that respect each of your screens.

The suite grows one app at a time. A new one starts from the suite's templates and passes just audit before its first release.

How they are made

Different apps, one way of making them

The apps share no code. They share how they are built, so an agent that has worked on one finds its way around the others. The conventions are written down in SUITE.md and checked by an audit.

$ just doctor# what this machine is missing
$ just dev# build it and run it
$ just fmt# format the sources
$ just ci# the gate, before every commit
$ just changelog# what changed since the last tag
$ just release# a signed release on GitHub
  • The gate is localNo hosted pipeline: just ci checks the commits, formatting, lints and tests on the author's machine.
  • Every release is signedEach file ships with its .sha256 and a minisign .minisig, under tags like pave-v0.6.0.
  • Decisions are written downEach app keeps its decision records in docs/adrs, and AGENTS.md tells an agent how to work on it.
  • Free to use and to forkEvery app is under MIT OR Apache-2.0, with Conventional Commits that become the release notes.
